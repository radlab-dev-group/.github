## Intro i Outro

We encourage you to take a look behind the scenes at how the [Information Browser](https://radlab.dev/2025/05/28/przegladarka-informacji/) and [Information Explorer](https://playground.radlab.dev/Eksplorator_Informacji) work 😉 For this occasion, we have prepared a technical report with a detailed description of how our solutions work. In the report, we present precise definitions of information and its types, i.e., *daily, continuous,* and *discontinuous information*. We have introduced definitions of information *graphs for information* and presented the process of their construction, analysis, and extraction of elements that make up the *information browser* and *information explorer*. The data that feeds these methods is, of course, *news* from the [News stream](https://playground.radlab.dev/Strumie%C5%84_Aktualno%C5%9Bci). We have also launched a public [GitHuba](https://github.com/radlab-dev-group/latex/blob/main/tech_reports/playground/przegladarka_eksplorator/REAMDE.md), where we have posted both the *[pdf](https://github.com/radlab-dev-group/latex/blob/main/tech_reports/playground/przegladarka_eksplorator/Eksplorator_Informacji-Raport-techniczny-latest.pdf)* and [LaTeX codes](https://github.com/radlab-dev-group/latex/tree/main/tech_reports/playground/przegladarka_eksplorator) of the report.
