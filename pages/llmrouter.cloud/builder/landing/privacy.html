Privacy policy

What this website measures, who runs it, and the rights you have. Last updated: 2026-10-08.

The short version: this site is a static page. It runs no ad networks, no cross-site trackers and no forms, and it collects nothing before you choose. If you accept, it loads one script — Google Analytics — which records basic, aggregate page statistics only. The llm-router software itself ships with zero telemetry; this policy covers the website, not the software.

01Who runs this site

The site is operated by the radlab-dev-group community, the maintainers of the open-source llm-router project. The website is published from a public GitHub repository and hosted for free on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.).

Questions about this policy can be sent to GitHub issues on the project repository.

04Legal basis

  • Storing and reading the analytics cookies needs your consent under the ePrivacy rules; the consent banner implements it and can be withdrawn at any time.
  • Once you consent, the processing rests on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in understanding basic, aggregate usage of a public project site so it can be kept useful. The processing is limited to that purpose.

If you are not in the EEA, the same minimal practices apply; where local law grants comparable rights (e.g. California), they apply to you too.

05Retention

We do not keep analytics data longer than necessary: Google Analytics deletes or anonymises the underlying event data after a 14-month retention window. Your consent choice stays in your browser until you change or clear it. Hosting logs kept by GitHub are governed by GitHub’s own privacy policy.

06Who we share data with

Only two parties ever see anything: Google, which processes the analytics events under its standard terms, and GitHub, which hosts this site and keeps basic access logs for security. We do not sell, rent or trade any data, and no advertising networks are involved.

07Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete anything we hold about you, to restrict or object to processing, or to hand the data over to another controller. Withdraw consent by clicking Change analytics consent on any page (or clearing site data). Requests go through the contact in section 01. If you believe something went wrong, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority — in Poland, the UODO.

08Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date above is updated and the change is published on this page. Material changes will be announced on the landing page.
